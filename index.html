Languages

Pick them in the two columns above the subtitles: “I speak” on the left, “They speak” on the right. These 24 languages interpret with each other — all but the few combinations listed below — with the direction worked out for you:

Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Hindi, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Arabic, Thai, Turkish, Filipino, Polish, Urdu, Hebrew, Persian, Malay, Dutch, Bangla

  • Bangla is subtitles only: what others say is shown in Bangla as text, not spoken; speaking Bangla still gets a spoken translation.

Common questions

No sound

Check the ring/silent switch above the volume buttons on the left edge — orange means silent. Then make sure the speech selector at the top is not set to “🔇 Voice off”, and that you are not translating into Bangla or Cantonese, which are subtitles only. If it is still quiet, turn the volume up: the earpiece and the speaker have separate volume levels.

Listening privately instead of out loud

Switch the speech selector at the top to 📞 Earpiece and the translation comes out of the receiver, held to your ear like a call. You can switch at any time, including mid-session.

“The system disconnected the microphone”

Usually another app took the microphone — a call, a voice message, a recorder — or a Bluetooth headset changed modes. Stop and start again to recover. If it keeps happening with Bluetooth, try the built-in microphone or a wired headset.

Howling or echo when using the speaker

In one room, the speaker's output gets picked up by the microphone again. Under Settings → Echo handling, choose “Lower mic during playback” or “Mute mic during playback”, or simply lower the volume. Using the earpiece avoids this entirely.

Subtitles stopped updating

The bar at the top shows the session's health. The usual causes are a dropped network or the system muting the microphone, which is reported explicitly. Stopping and starting again normally clears it. With bought time, check the time left at the top; with your own key, a quota problem from Alibaba Cloud is passed through as-is.

Moving to a new phone

Bought time belongs to your account, a random ID the app creates and keeps in iCloud Keychain: sign in to the same Apple Account on the new phone with iCloud Keychain on, and the balance is there. “All languages”, bought when using your own key, comes back under Settings → All languages → Restore purchases at no charge.

Why Cantonese ⇄ Mandarin only speaks one way

The model can understand and write Cantonese, but not speak it — in the provider's language table it is subtitles-only. So speaking Cantonese gives you spoken Mandarin; speaking Mandarin gives you Cantonese subtitles only. Both sides use Chinese characters, so the direction cannot be detected automatically — set it with the direction selector.

Direction occasionally flips, e.g. between Spanish and Portuguese

When both languages share an alphabet (English, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, French, German, Indonesian, Vietnamese and so on), detection goes by word choice and can waver on short phrases or names. These combinations get an extra direction control at the top — switch it from “Direction · automatic” to a fixed direction to pin it.
