常见问题

Common questions

没有声音 No sound

先看 iPhone 左侧上方的静音拨片有没有拨到静音（露出橙色）。 再确认界面右上角的「译音」没有选成「译音关」。还是没有的话，把音量调大一些—— 听筒和外放用的是两套独立的音量刻度。

Check the ring/silent switch above the volume buttons on the left edge — orange means silent. Then make sure the speech selector at the top right is not set to off. If it is still quiet, turn the volume up: the earpiece and the speaker have separate volume levels.

想贴着耳朵听，不想外放 Listening privately instead of out loud

把顶部的「译音」切到 📞 听筒，译音就从听筒出声，像打电话一样贴耳听。 传译进行中也可以随时切换。

Switch the speech selector at the top to 📞 Earpiece and the translation comes out of the receiver, held to your ear like a call. You can switch at any time, including mid-session.

提示「麦克风已被系统断开」 “The system disconnected the microphone”

通常是别的应用抢走了麦克风（来电、语音消息、录音类 App），或蓝牙耳机切换了模式。 停止后重新开始即可恢复。如果插着蓝牙耳机反复出现，换成内置麦克风或有线耳机试试。

Usually another app took the microphone — a call, a voice message, a recorder — or a Bluetooth headset changed modes. Stop and start again to recover. If it keeps happening with Bluetooth, try the built-in microphone or a wired headset.

外放时出现啸叫或回音 Howling or echo when using the speaker

同一个房间里外放，扬声器的译音会被麦克风重新拾到。 在设置 → 回声处理里改成「播放译音时压低麦克风」或「静音麦克风」， 也可以把扬声器音量调低一些。改用听筒则完全不会有这个问题。

In one room, the speaker's output gets picked up by the microphone again. Under Settings → Echo handling, choose to duck or mute the microphone while the translation plays, or simply lower the volume. Using the earpiece avoids this entirely.

字幕停住不动了 Subtitles stopped updating

界面顶部会显示健康状态。常见原因是网络中断，或麦克风被系统静音—— 后者会有明确提示。停止再开始通常就好；如果服务商那边报额度问题，界面上会原样转述它的错误信息。

The bar at the top shows the session's health. The usual causes are a dropped network or the system muting the microphone, which is reported explicitly. Stopping and starting again normally clears it; if the provider reports a quota problem, its message is passed through as-is.

换了手机，之前买的语言对怎么办 Moving to a new phone

用同一个 Apple ID 登录，打开设置 → 语言对解锁 → 恢复购买即可全部取回。 买断是永久的，不会二次收费。

Sign in with the same Apple ID and use Settings → Language pairs → Restore purchases. Purchases are permanent; you are never charged twice.

某些语言对为什么标着「仅字幕」 Why some pairs say “subtitles only”

火山引擎在中韩、中泰和两种方言上没有语音合成模型，只能出字幕。 同样是中韩，切到 OpenAI 后端就有译音——所以这个标记是跟着后端变的。

Volcengine has no speech model for Chinese with Korean or Thai, nor for the two dialects, so those produce subtitles only. The same Chinese ⇄ Korean pair does have speech on the OpenAI backend, which is why the label changes with the backend.

英语和西班牙语之间，方向判断偶尔会反 Direction occasionally flips between English and Spanish

两种语言用同一套字母，判别只能靠用词，短句或专有名词上可能拿不准。 这类语言对的顶部会多出一个方向开关，把它从「自动」改成固定方向即可按住。

Those two share an alphabet, so detection goes by word choice and can waver on short phrases or names. Pairs like this get an extra direction control at the top — switch it from automatic to a fixed direction to pin it.
