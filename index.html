支持的语言

Languages

在字幕上方的两栏里选：左边「我说」，右边「对方说」。下面 24 种语言之间都能互译（只有下面列出的几组除外）， 方向自动识别：

Pick them in the two columns above the subtitles: “I speak” on the left, “They speak” on the right. These 24 languages interpret with each other — all but the few combinations listed below — with the direction worked out for you:

中文、英语、日语、韩语、德语、法语、西班牙语、葡萄牙语、意大利语、俄语、印地语、印尼语、越南语、 阿拉伯语、泰语、土耳其语、菲律宾语、波兰语、乌尔都语、希伯来语、波斯语、马来语、荷兰语、孟加拉语

Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Hindi, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Arabic, Thai, Turkish, Filipino, Polish, Urdu, Hebrew, Persian, Malay, Dutch, Bangla

  • 孟加拉语只有字幕：别人的话译成孟加拉语时只出字幕、没有译音；说孟加拉语照样有译音。
  • Bangla is subtitles only: what others say is shown in Bangla as text, not spoken; speaking Bangla still gets a spoken translation.
  • 不配对的几组：印尼语与马来语、印地语与乌尔都语（口语基本相通），以及阿拉伯语、乌尔都语、波斯语彼此之间 （同用阿拉伯字母，暂时分不出是谁在说）。
  • A few combinations are left out: Indonesian with Malay and Hindi with Urdu (people already understand each other), and Arabic, Urdu and Persian with each other (same alphabet; the app cannot yet tell who is speaking).
  • 粤语 ⇄ 普通话——说粤语有译音，反过来只有字幕。
  • Cantonese ⇄ Mandarin — spoken one way, subtitles the other.

常见问题

Common questions

没有声音No sound

先看 iPhone 左侧上方的静音拨片有没有拨到静音（露出橙色）。 再确认界面顶部的「译音」没有选成「译音关」，以及译成的那种语言不是只有字幕的孟加拉语或粤语。还是没有的话，把音量调大一些—— 听筒和外放用的是两套独立的音量刻度。

Check the ring/silent switch above the volume buttons on the left edge — orange means silent. Then make sure the speech selector at the top is not set to off, and that you are not translating into Bangla or Cantonese, which are subtitles only. If it is still quiet, turn the volume up: the earpiece and the speaker have separate volume levels.

想贴着耳朵听，不想外放Listening privately instead of out loud

把顶部的「译音」切到 📞 听筒，译音就从听筒出声，像打电话一样贴耳听。 传译进行中也可以随时切换。

Switch the speech selector at the top to 📞 Earpiece and the translation comes out of the receiver, held to your ear like a call. You can switch at any time, including mid-session.

提示「麦克风已被系统断开」“The system disconnected the microphone”

通常是别的应用抢走了麦克风（来电、语音消息、录音类 App），或蓝牙耳机切换了模式。 停止后重新开始即可恢复。如果插着蓝牙耳机反复出现，换成内置麦克风或有线耳机试试。

Usually another app took the microphone — a call, a voice message, a recorder — or a Bluetooth headset changed modes. Stop and start again to recover. If it keeps happening with Bluetooth, try the built-in microphone or a wired headset.

外放时出现啸叫或回音Howling or echo when using the speaker

同一个房间里外放，扬声器的译音会被麦克风重新拾到。 在设置 → 回声处理里改成「播放译音时压低麦克风」或「静音麦克风」， 也可以把扬声器音量调低一些。改用听筒则完全不会有这个问题。

In one room, the speaker's output gets picked up by the microphone again. Under Settings → Echo handling, choose to duck or mute the microphone while the translation plays, or simply lower the volume. Using the earpiece avoids this entirely.

字幕停住不动了Subtitles stopped updating

界面顶部会显示健康状态。常见原因是网络中断，或麦克风被系统静音—— 后者会有明确提示。停止再开始通常就好；买时长的话看看顶部的剩余时长；自带 Key 时如果阿里云报额度问题，界面上会原样转述它的错误信息。

The bar at the top shows the session's health. The usual causes are a dropped network or the system muting the microphone, which is reported explicitly. Stopping and starting again normally clears it. With bought time, check the time left at the top; with your own key, a quota problem from Alibaba Cloud is passed through as-is.

换了手机，买过的时长和语言怎么办Moving to a new phone

买的时长记在你的账号上。账号是 App 自动生成的随机 ID，存在 iCloud 钥匙串里： 新手机登录同一个 Apple 账户、打开 iCloud 钥匙串，余额就在。 自带 Key 时买的「全部语言」，在设置 → 全部语言 → 恢复购买里取回，不会二次收费。

Bought time belongs to your account, a random ID the app creates and keeps in iCloud Keychain: sign in to the same Apple Account on the new phone with iCloud Keychain on, and the balance is there. “All languages”, bought when using your own key, comes back under Settings → All languages → Restore purchases at no charge.

「粤 ⇄ 普」为什么反过来没有声音Why Cantonese ⇄ Mandarin only speaks one way

模型能听懂粤语，也能写粤语，但没法说粤语—— 它在官方语言表里属于「只有字幕」那一档。所以说粤语时会听到普通话译音；说普通话时， 粤语译文只会以字幕出现。这一对两边都是汉字，方向自动判不出来，请用方向开关手动指定。

The model can understand and write Cantonese, but not speak it — in the provider's language table it is subtitles-only. So speaking Cantonese gives you spoken Mandarin; speaking Mandarin gives you Cantonese subtitles only. Both sides use Chinese characters, so the direction cannot be detected automatically — set it with the direction selector.

西班牙语和葡萄牙语这类组合，方向偶尔会判反Direction occasionally flips, e.g. between Spanish and Portuguese

两种语言用同一套字母时（英、西、葡、意、法、德、印尼、越南语等之间），判别只能靠用词， 短句或专有名词上可能拿不准。这类组合的顶部会多出一个方向开关，把它从「自动」改成固定方向即可按住。

When both languages share an alphabet (English, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, French, German, Indonesian, Vietnamese and so on), detection goes by word choice and can waver on short phrases or names. These combinations get an extra direction control at the top — switch it from automatic to a fixed direction to pin it.
