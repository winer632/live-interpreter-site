隐私政策

Privacy Policy

实时同声传译 · 最后更新 2026 年 9 月 17 日

Duo Interpreter · Last updated 17 September 2026

一句话版本：这个 App 没有服务器、不需要注册、不做任何统计。 你的语音直接从手机发往你自己配置的翻译服务商，我们全程不经手、也无从留存。 字幕记录只存在你的手机里。

In short: this app has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. Your speech goes straight from your phone to the translation provider you configured yourself; it never passes through us and we have nothing to keep. Transcripts stay on your phone.

我们收集什么

What we collect

什么都不收集。我们不运营任何服务器，没有账号体系，App 里也没有第三方分析、 广告或跟踪组件。我们无法知道有谁在用、用了多久、翻译了什么。

Nothing. We run no servers, there are no accounts, and the app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking. We cannot tell who uses it, for how long, or what was said.

你的语音去了哪里

Where your speech goes

传译进行时，麦克风采集的音频会实时发送给你在设置里选定的翻译服务商， 用的是你自己的 API Key，连接由手机直连，不经过我们：

While interpreting, the audio from your microphone is sent live to whichever translation provider you picked in settings, using your own API key, directly from your phone:

  • 火山引擎 · 同声传译（openspeech.bytedance.com）—— 其隐私条款
  • Volcengine Simultaneous Interpretation (openspeech.bytedance.com) — their terms
  • 通义千问 · LiveTranslate（dashscope-intl.aliyuncs.com 或 dashscope.aliyuncs.com）—— 其隐私政策
  • Qwen LiveTranslate (dashscope-intl.aliyuncs.com or dashscope.aliyuncs.com) — their privacy policy

这些服务商如何处理这段音频，取决于你和他们之间的协议，请以他们的隐私政策为准。 App 里除这两个之外不向任何其他地址发送数据。

What those providers do with the audio is governed by your agreement with them, so read their policies. The app sends data to no other address.

演示模式完全离线：它播放的是随 App 打包的预录音频，不联网、不用麦克风。

Demo mode is fully offline: it plays audio bundled with the app, with no network and no microphone.

留在你手机上的东西

What stays on your phone

字幕记录

Transcripts

每句定稿的字幕会按天存成文件，放在 App 自己的沙盒目录里，不会上传， 也不参与 iCloud 备份。可以在 App 的「记录」里查看、导出或删除；卸载 App 即随之清除。

Finished lines are written to one file per day inside the app's own container. They are never uploaded and are excluded from iCloud backup. You can read, export or delete them under “Transcripts” in the app; deleting the app removes them.

API Key

API keys

你填写的 API Key 保存在 iOS 钥匙串中，标记为「仅本机、解锁后可读」， 既不上传也不随备份迁移到其他设备。

The API keys you enter are kept in the iOS Keychain, marked as readable only on this device and only while it is unlocked. They are never uploaded and do not migrate to another device via backup.

麦克风

Microphone

只有在你点击「开始传译」之后才会使用麦克风，点击「停止」或 App 退到后台即停止采集。 系统状态栏的橙色指示点可以随时印证这一点。

The microphone is used only after you press Start, and capture stops when you press Stop or the app goes to the background. The system's orange indicator dot shows this at all times.

购买

Purchases

语言对的解锁通过 Apple 的 App 内购买完成。支付全过程由 Apple 处理， 我们收不到也看不到你的支付方式、姓名或账单信息；App 只向系统询问「这个语言对是否已购买」。

Language pairs are unlocked through Apple's in-app purchase. Apple handles payment end to end; we never receive or see your payment method, name or billing details. The app only asks the system whether a given pair has been bought.

儿童

Children

本 App 面向商务与日常口译场景，不面向儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的任何信息。

This app is aimed at business and everyday interpreting. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect anything from them.

政策变更

Changes

如果这份政策有实质性变化，我们会更新页面顶部的日期。由于 App 不收集数据、也没有账号， 我们无法主动通知你，请以本页为准。

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top. Since the app collects nothing and has no accounts, we have no way to notify you directly — this page is the record.
