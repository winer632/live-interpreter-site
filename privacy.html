一句话版本：App 有两种用法。默认的「购买时长」不用注册：你的语音从手机直接发往阿里云百炼翻译， 不经过我们的服务器；我们的服务器只记一个随机生成的账号号码、你买了多少时长、用了多少时长。 选「用自己的阿里云 Key」时，我们什么都不收集。App 里没有统计、广告和追踪，字幕记录只存在你的手机里。

In short: the app works in two ways. By default you buy interpreting time, with no sign-up: your speech goes straight from your phone to Alibaba Cloud Model Studio for translation and never passes through our server; our server only keeps a randomly generated account number, how much time you bought and how much you used. If you use your own Alibaba Cloud key instead, we collect nothing. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking, and transcripts stay on your phone.

购买时长（默认，免注册）

Buying time (the default, no sign-up)

为了记住你买了多少时长、防止被盗刷，App 会连接我们的服务器 api.duo-interpreter.com （部署在 Microsoft Azure 新加坡区域）。服务器保存这些：

To remember how much time you bought and to stop abuse, the app talks to our server api.duo-interpreter.com (hosted on Microsoft Azure in Singapore). The server keeps:

  • 随机账号 ID：App 第一次用时随机生成的一串号码（UUID），和你的姓名、邮箱、电话、Apple 账户都没有关系。 它存在你的 iCloud 钥匙串里，所以重装 App、换同一 Apple 账户的新手机，余额都还在。
  • A random account ID: a number (a UUID) the app generates the first time you use it. It has nothing to do with your name, email, phone number or Apple Account. It is kept in your iCloud Keychain, so your balance survives reinstalls and follows you to new phones on the same Apple Account.
  • 设备凭证：每台设备一把 Apple App Attest 密钥的编号和公钥，以及登记时间、最后一次使用时间。 用来确认请求来自这个 App 的正版安装，而不是被篡改的程序。
  • Device credentials: for each device, the identifier and public key of an Apple App Attest key, with when it was registered and last used. They prove that requests come from a genuine copy of this app, not a modified one.
  • 购买记录：Apple 的交易号、商品（1、5 或 10 小时）、购买时间、是否是测试（沙盒）购买、是否已退款。
  • Purchases: Apple's transaction ID, the product (1, 5 or 10 hours), when it was bought, whether it was a test (sandbox) purchase, and whether it was refunded.
  • 使用记录：每次传译的开始和结束时间、模式（念出译文或只出字幕）、扣了多少时长、这次传译向服务器要了几次临时密钥， 以及余额的每一笔增减。
  • Usage: when each interpreting session started and ended, its mode (spoken translation or subtitles only), how much time it used, how many temporary keys it asked for, and every change to your balance.

新设备送的 5 分钟试用每台设备只送一次。为了防止重复领，App 会把 Apple DeviceCheck 生成的一次性令牌交给服务器， 服务器拿它请 Apple 查一下、再标记 Apple 替我们给这台设备保存的 1 个比特（「领过试用」）。令牌用完即弃，服务器不保存； Apple 只替我们记这 1 个比特，不会告诉我们这是哪台设备。

The 5-minute trial is given once per device. To prevent repeat claims the app hands the server a one-time Apple DeviceCheck token; the server uses it to ask Apple to check, then set, a single bit Apple keeps for this device on our behalf (“trial used”). The token is used once and not stored, and Apple keeps only that bit for us, without telling us which device it is.

服务器收不到：你的语音、字幕和翻译内容，你的姓名、邮箱、电话、位置、通讯录，以及你的付款方式。

The server never receives your speech, subtitles or translations, your name, email, phone number, location or contacts, or your payment details.

服务器日志与保存期限

Server logs and retention

连接服务器时，你的 IP 地址只在内存里用来限制请求频率（防刷），最后一次请求一小时后清掉，不写日志、不进数据库。 服务器日志只记事件，比如「有一台设备登记」（只记账号 ID 的前 8 位）、「一笔购买入账」（商品和是否是测试购买）、 「一笔退款」（交易号），以及出错信息。服务器日志保留 30 天后自动删除。

Your IP address is used only in memory to limit request rates (to stop abuse) and is dropped an hour after your last request; it is not logged or stored. Server logs record events only, such as “a device registered” (with just the first 8 characters of the account ID), “a purchase was credited” (the product and whether it was a test purchase), “a refund was applied” (the transaction ID), and errors. Server logs are deleted automatically after 30 days.

账本每小时在服务器上做一份快照，保留最近 48 份；另外加密后传到异地备份，保留 30 天。账号、购买和使用记录在账号存续期间一直保留， 对账和处理 Apple 的退款都要用到。

The ledger is snapshotted on the server every hour, keeping the latest 48, and an encrypted copy is kept off-site for 30 days. Account, purchase and usage records are kept for as long as the account exists, because they are needed to reconcile the books and to handle Apple refunds.

你的语音去了哪里

Where your speech goes

两种用法下，传译时麦克风采集的音频都从手机直接实时发往阿里云百炼（通义千问 LiveTranslate）， 不经过我们的服务器，我们听不到，也存不下：

In both modes, the audio from your microphone goes live and directly from your phone to Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Qwen LiveTranslate). It never passes through our server; we cannot hear it and have nothing to keep:

  • 购买时长时，连接的是 dashscope-intl.aliyuncs.com（新加坡），用的是我们的阿里云账户： 服务器只给 App 签发一把 60 秒内有效的临时密钥去连接，音频按我们和阿里云之间的协议处理。
  • When you buy time, the app connects to dashscope-intl.aliyuncs.com (Singapore) on our Alibaba Cloud account: our server only issues the app a temporary key that expires after 60 seconds, and the audio is handled under our agreement with Alibaba Cloud.
  • 用自己的 Key 时，连接你在设置里选的站点（dashscope-intl.aliyuncs.com 或 dashscope.aliyuncs.com）， 用的是你自己的 Key，音频按你和阿里云之间的协议处理。
  • With your own key, the app connects to the site you picked in settings (dashscope-intl.aliyuncs.com or dashscope.aliyuncs.com) with your key, and the audio is handled under your agreement with Alibaba Cloud.
  • 阿里云如何处理音频，见 其隐私政策。
  • For what Alibaba Cloud does with the audio, see their privacy policy.

App 只连接这几处：阿里云百炼、我们的服务器（仅购买时长时），以及 Apple 的服务（App 内购买、App Attest、DeviceCheck）。

The app connects only to Alibaba Cloud Model Studio, our server (only when buying time), and Apple's services (in-app purchase, App Attest, DeviceCheck).

译音用说话人的声音（默认开启，可在设置里关闭）：阿里云用同一段语音实时模仿说话人的音色来念译文， 每句跟着当时说话的人走，双方说话时都是如此。App 和我们都不另外采集、保存或上传声纹，用的就是本来发去翻译的那段音频。

Translations in the speaker's voice (on by default, can be turned off in settings): Alibaba Cloud imitates the speaker's voice live from the same audio it translates, following whoever is speaking — both people in a conversation. Neither the app nor we separately collect, store or upload any voiceprint; it is the same audio already sent for translation.

留在你手机上的东西

What stays on your phone

字幕记录

Transcripts

每句定稿的字幕会按天存成文件，放在 App 自己的沙盒目录里，不会上传， 也不参与 iCloud 备份。可以在 App 的「记录」里查看、导出或删除；卸载 App 即随之清除。

Finished lines are written to one file per day inside the app's own container. They are never uploaded and are excluded from iCloud backup. You can read, export or delete them under “Transcripts” in the app; deleting the app removes them.

账号 ID

Account ID

上面说的随机账号 ID 存在 iOS 钥匙串里，打开了 iCloud 钥匙串时会在你同一 Apple 账户的设备间同步（由 Apple 端到端加密）。 卸载 App 不会删掉它，重装后余额还在。

The random account ID described above is kept in the iOS Keychain and, if iCloud Keychain is on, syncs between your devices on the same Apple Account (end-to-end encrypted by Apple). Deleting the app does not remove it, so your balance is still there after a reinstall.

API Key

API keys

你自己填写的 API Key 保存在 iOS 钥匙串中，标记为「仅本机、解锁后可读」， 既不上传也不随备份迁移到其他设备。

The API keys you enter yourself are kept in the iOS Keychain, marked as readable only on this device and only while it is unlocked. They are never uploaded and do not migrate to another device via backup.

麦克风

Microphone

只有在你点击「开始传译」之后才会使用麦克风。切换到其他 App 或锁屏后，正在进行的传译会继续采集、翻译并保存字幕； 点击「停止」结束采集。来电等系统音频中断会暂停会话，系统允许恢复时继续。系统的橙色麦克风指示点会显示采集状态。

The microphone is used only after you press Start. An active session continues recording, translating and saving transcripts when you switch apps or lock the screen. Press Stop to end capture. System audio interruptions, such as calls, pause the session; it resumes when the system permits. The system's orange microphone indicator shows when capture is active.

购买

Purchases

购买时长和「全部语言」（用自己的 Key 时）都通过 Apple 的 App 内购买完成。支付全过程由 Apple 处理， 我们收不到也看不到你的支付方式、姓名或账单信息。

Buying time and “All languages” (when using your own key) both go through Apple's in-app purchase. Apple handles payment end to end; we never receive or see your payment method, name or billing details.

  • 购买时长：App 把账号 ID 交给 Apple 写进这笔交易，再把 Apple 签名的交易凭证交给我们的服务器核验、入账； 服务器只给交易里写明的那个账号加时长。退款由 Apple 处理，Apple 会通知服务器扣回相应时长。
  • Buying time: the app asks Apple to record your account ID in the transaction, then sends the transaction, signed by Apple, to our server to verify and credit; the server credits only the account named in the transaction. Refunds are handled by Apple, which notifies our server to take the time back.
  • 「全部语言」：App 只向系统询问「是否已购买」，不经过我们的服务器。
  • “All languages”: the app only asks the system whether it has been bought; our server is not involved.

查询与删除

Access and deletion

你的账号 ID 在 App 的「设置 → 剩余时长」里，可以复制。想查询或删除服务器上关于你的记录，把它写信发到 winer632@qq.com，我们会删除这个账号在服务器上的全部记录 （快照和异地备份在各自的保留期后过期）。删除后剩余时长无法恢复。购买的退款请向 Apple 申请。

Your account ID is under Settings → Time left in the app, with a Copy button. To see or delete what our server holds about you, send it to winer632@qq.com and we will delete all of that account’s records on the server (snapshots and the off-site copy expire after their retention periods). Remaining time cannot be restored after deletion. Refunds for purchases are requested from Apple.
